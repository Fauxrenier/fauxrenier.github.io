About Journey Fast

Journey Fast is a fasting timer designed to help users track fasting sessions, monitor progress, and build healthier habits.

Features include:

  • Custom fasting goals
  • Progress tracking
  • Fasting history
  • Goal notifications
  • Automatic fasting session extensions

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Journey Fast require an account?

No. Journey Fast works without creating an account.

Is my data stored online?

No. Your fasting history and settings are stored locally on your device.

Will I lose my data if I uninstall the app?

Yes. Because data is currently stored locally, uninstalling the app may remove your fasting history.

Why am I not receiving notifications?

Please verify that notifications are enabled for Journey Fast in your device settings.

Can I edit fasting history?

Yes. Tap a fasting session in the History screen to view available actions.
