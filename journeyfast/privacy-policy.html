Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

Journey Fast respects your privacy.

Information We Collect

Journey Fast stores fasting session information that you choose to create within the app, including fasting start times, fasting end times, fasting duration history, and app preferences.

Local Storage

All fasting data and settings are stored locally on your device. Journey Fast does not require an account and does not transmit your fasting data to external servers.

Analytics

Journey Fast does not currently use analytics, tracking tools, advertising networks, or third-party services to collect personal information.

Notifications

Journey Fast may use device notification features to remind you about fasting goals. These notifications are handled by your device operating system.

Data Sharing

Journey Fast does not sell, share, rent, or distribute your personal information.

Children's Privacy

Journey Fast is not directed toward children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page.
